What this is built on

Shanten, draw counts, waits and the next-step lookahead are computed exactly from the tiles you enter. Deal-in percentages come from the turn-by-turn tables in Statistical Mahjong Strategy (Tenhou game logs), adjusted for sotogawa, suji traps, the riichi tile, discarded fives, one-chance and no-chance walls, ankou suji and dora. Expected losses use that book’s average hand values. Push/fold combines its round-balance results with The Modern Mahjong Push-Pull Textbook and Yuusei’s lectures. Opponent speed levels follow Yuusei’s discard speed-reading, and open-hand reads follow Double Your Safe Tiles. The analog read applies principles from all the readable books. The Digital pick was checked against 27 answered problems from the wwyd-chan books: Kawa’s acceptance counts matched the books on every one, and its pick agrees with the authors’ answer on 14 of 27 (the author’s answer is in its top two on 18). The authors pick the widest acceptance only 15 times out of 27, preferring good-shape tenpai, tanyao and dora; the Most efficient card shows the pure-acceptance answer. The SMS tables were built from games with red fives, so with red fives off the deal-in rates are close but hand values run lower. Photos can be misread, so always check the tiles.
